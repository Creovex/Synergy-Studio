# Short-form craft (ads, reels, explainers)

These are what usually works, learned from platforms and from the videos in this repository. Use them
as defaults and break them on purpose when the idea is stronger for it (creative.md).

## The first 3 seconds decide everything
Most viewers decide to stay or scroll in 1–3 s. The hook is in the **first frame** (no logo intro, no
fade from black, no "Hi guys"), and it works **with the sound off** (most people watch muted).
Hook types that work:
| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Problem they feel | "Tired by 3 p.m.?" |
| Counter-intuitive claim | "Posting more is killing your reach." |
| Result promise | "The 3-step check that stops fake medicine." |
| Secret / decoded | "Real estate listings speak their own secret language." |
| Person + stakes | "Meet the face behind HAURA SCENT." |
| Number list | "9 Lagos areas on the 2026 flood alerts." |
Write 3 hook options in the plan and pick the strongest; the hook's words appear on screen as text.

## Timing templates
**15 s ad:** 0–2 hook · 2–10 product/benefit (2–3 shots) · 10–15 offer + CTA.
**30 s:** 0–3 hook · 3–8 problem or promise · 8–24 value (3–4 beats) · 24–30 CTA.
**60 s:** 0–3 hook · 3–15 problem/promise · 15–50 value (tips, steps, proof) · 50–60 CTA.
**Long-form (> 90 s):** hook 0–30 s, context to 90 s, body in chapters, one CTA in the last minute.
Change something on screen every 1–2 s in social cuts, every 2–4 s in calm explainers.

## Numbers
Count up big numbers (37 tools, 96%): the motion makes them feel earned. Small offer numbers (5% OFF) should
**land** instead (a scale-in with a sound), fully readable the moment they are said; a counter would show
"4%" while the voice says "five".

## One idea per video
A short sells one thing, teaches one tip-set, or tells one story. A second idea is a second video.

## Copy on screen
- ≤ 12 words on screen at once; headlines 3–7 words; active voice; numbers as digits on screen.
- Say it and show it together (the same words, or the key noun, appear as it is spoken).
- Ads and brand posts: brand name and handle appear at least twice (hook area or middle, and the end card).
- Captions on for anything with speech: 2–4 words per caption line, active word highlighted.

## Endings (CTA)
One action, said and shown: Follow · Shop now / DM / WhatsApp to order · Save this · Link in bio ·
Visit <site>. Hold the end card at least 2 s. Offers state the exact terms (5% off all perfumes, until <date>).

## Platform specs
| Platform | Format | Length that works | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 9:16, 1080×1920 | 15–60 s | captions on; keep the bottom 22% and right 12% clear |
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 | 15–60 s | same safe areas; a 1:1 or 4:5 cut for the feed |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16 | ≤ 60 s | a searchable title with the main keyword |
| YouTube | 16:9, 1920×1080 | 1–10 min | chapters for > 6 min; keyword in the first 30 s |
| LinkedIn | 1:1 or 16:9 | 30–90 s | professional framing, captions, text overlay |
| X | 16:9 or 1:1 | ≤ 45 s | captions |
Never post a 16:9 video to a vertical platform without re-editing it to 9:16.

## Packaging (give this with every delivery)
Post caption (hook line + 1–2 lines of value + CTA), 3–5 hashtags specific to the niche (not generic),
a thumbnail/cover frame time, and the "AI-generated" label reminder when a synthetic voice or images are used.

## Common mistakes (mention them to the user if you see them in their request)
| Don't | Why | Do |
|---|---|---|
| Logo or title card first | viewers scroll before the point | open on the hook; brand at the end and in a corner |
| Several ideas in one short | unfocused, low retention | one idea; split the rest into more videos |
| Tiny or fast text | unreadable on phones | design.md sizes; hold 0.25 s per word + 0.6 s |
| Transitions with no reason (whips, flashes, spins) | reads as cheap (the HAURA v1 was rejected for this) | cuts, dissolves, slow push-ins; or a motivated move (cinema.md §4) |
| Music louder than the voice | words get lost | the mix ducks music about 8 dB under voice; keep it |
| A CTA buried or missing | nobody acts | one clear action, said and shown, last 3–5 s |
| Invented numbers or claims | trust and legal risk | only sourced facts; otherwise cut |
| Reposting horizontal video to Reels/TikTok | small picture, letterboxing, weaker reach | re-edit to 9:16 with captions |
