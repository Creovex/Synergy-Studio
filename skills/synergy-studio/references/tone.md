# Tone: make every video feel like its own brief

Two briefs that share a word are not the same brief. "Luxury and fun" and "luxury and calm" share
"luxury" but should differ in colour, motion, type, music, voice and words. Unless the user asks for a
series look, **no two videos should look, sound or feel the same**. The looks, helpers and default
music exist to save time, not to set the style.

## 1. Write a tone card before designing (in brief.md)
Take 3–5 tone words from the user (or propose them). Give each word:
- what it **is**, in every channel (the table in §2);
- what it must **not** become, the failure mode next door.

| Word | Is | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Luxury | rare, unhurried, precise, confident | cold, stiff, empty, boring |
| Fun | surprising, bouncy, bright, playful | childish, noisy, cheap |
| Calm | slow, spacious, soft | sleepy, flat, lifeless |
| Bold | big, high contrast, direct | shouting, cluttered |
| Warm | human, soft light, close | cheesy, beige |
| Premium tech | clean, exact, glowing detail | generic SaaS gradient |

Then name one **signature move** that only this video will have: a gesture, a transition, a sound or a
colour moment. It should be the thing people remember.

## 2. Translate tone into every channel
| Channel | Luxury + calm | Luxury + fun | Fun + loud | Warm + human | Epic / cinematic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Palette | near-black, ivory, one metallic; low saturation | deep jewel base + one playful pop (fuchsia, electric blue) | saturated primaries, clashing on purpose | honey, terracotta, soft cream | deep shadows, one hot light colour |
| Type | high-contrast serif, wide tracking, small and centred | serif headline with a bouncy sans accent; words that tilt or swing | chunky rounded sans, huge, stacked | friendly humanist sans, handwritten notes | condensed caps, slow reveals |
| Composition | lots of negative space, one object, symmetry | asymmetric, objects entering from the edges, confetti used sparingly | full frame, stickers, overlaps | close framing, people and hands | extreme wides and close-ups |
| Motion | slow push-ins, long dissolves, 0.8–1.5 s eases, nothing bounces | elastic pops with a luxurious finish, a spin that settles, a wink | fast, snappy, squash and stretch, shakes on beats | gentle sway, handheld drift | slow build, then one huge move |
| Pacing (shot length) | 3–5 s | 1–2 s, with one long hold for the hero | 0.5–1.5 s | 2–4 s | 4–8 s building to 1 s cuts |
| Texture and light | soft grain, glints on metal, candle warmth | glossy highlights, sparkles that pop on the beat | flat colour, halftone, stickers | window light, paper, film grain | volumetric light, dust, lens flare |
| Music | sparse piano or strings, lots of silence | a swinging bassline, pizzicato, finger snaps with class | a punchy 120+ BPM beat, big drops | acoustic guitar, soft percussion | low drones, rising strings, hits |
| Sound effects | a single chime, a glass tap, cloth | playful pops tuned to the music, a champagne cork | cartoon boings, whooshes, record scratches | real room sounds | booms, risers |
| Voice | `bf_emma` or `af_heart` at 0.85–0.9, short lines, pauses | a smiling read at 1.0, a wink in the words | energetic at 1.05, or no voice (on-screen text) | conversational at 0.95 | deep `am_adam` at 0.9, or none |
| Words | few, exact, sensory ("Hand-poured. Yours.") | witty, a little cheeky, still elegant ("Dangerously well-dressed.") | shouty, fun, emoji-free on screen | plain, kind, first person | short, big claims made true |

These are starting points, not presets. Mix columns ("warm + epic") and keep inventing.

## 3. Make it not look like the last one
Before building, list the look of the last 2–3 videos in this workspace (their `brief.md` style card, their
stills). Change at least three of: palette, type family, layout system, motion vocabulary, music style,
voice, and texture or medium (vector, canvas drawing, photo, 3D). The only exceptions are a brand's fixed
identity and a series the user asked for: keep the identity, and vary everything else.

Watch for your own habits: the same centred title, the same rise/pop, the same warm music, the same
cream paper. When you notice one, do the opposite once and see whether the video gets better.

## 4. Check it
Look at the stills muted: would a stranger name the tone words from the brief? Then listen with the picture
off: would they name the same words? If either answer is no, change the channel that disagrees (review.md ◇ 28).
