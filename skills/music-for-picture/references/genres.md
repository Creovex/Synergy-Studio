# Genre recipes (starting points, not rules)

Grids are one 4/4 bar in 16 steps: `x` = hit, `o` = soft or ghost hit, `.` = rest. Step 1 is the downbeat;
steps 5, 9 and 13 are beats 2, 3 and 4. Program numbers are General MIDI, 0-based (gm-map.md). Vary every 4 or 8
bars: drop the kick for a bar, add a fill on the last beat, open the hat into the next section.

## Afrobeats (Afropop) (`afrobeats`) · 100–112 BPM · straight 16ths · Standard kit
```
kick    x.....x.x.......      rim/clap  x..x..x.x..x..x.   (3-3-2 tresillo, twice per bar)
shaker  oxoxoxoxoxoxoxox      congas    ..x....x..x...o.
```
- **Bass:** Fingered Bass 33 or Synth Bass 38; short syncopated notes on the kick, root and fifth, slides into bar 1.
- **Chords:** Tine EP 4 or a clean guitar 27 in offbeat skanks (steps 3, 7, 11, 15). Major keys with ii–V–I–vi, or a
  minor i–VI–III–VII loop.
- **Lead and colour:** kalimba 108, marimba 12, flute 73, brass stabs 61 on the "and"s, a whistle or call-and-response hook.

## Amapiano (`amapiano`) · 110–115 BPM · straight · Standard kit (sparse)
```
kick    x...x...x...x...  (often only on 1 and 3, or dropped)
clap    ....x.......x...      shaker    xoxoxoxoxoxoxoxo     rim  ..x...x.......x.
```
- **Log drum:** the signature. Synth Bass 38/39 (or a synth sine with a pitch glide), short notes with a slide down,
  playing a syncopated melody, not just roots.
- **Keys:** jazzy piano 0 or EP 4: min9, maj9 and 11th chords, held; soft pad 89 underneath.

## Highlife (`highlife`) · 120–130 BPM · bright and major · Standard kit or hand percussion
```
bell    x.x.xx.x.x.x.x..  (cowbell 56 or agogo 67)      kick  x.......x.......
conga   ..x.x..x..x.x..x
```
- Two interlocking clean guitars 27 with repeating 16th patterns in thirds, a walking bass 33, and horns 61 answering.

## Boom bap hip-hop (`boom-bap`) · 85–95 BPM · swing 56–60% · Standard or Room kit (bank 128 preset 0 or 8)
```
kick    x......x..x.....      snare  ....x.......x...      hat  x.x.x.x.x.x.x.xo
```
- Piano 0 or EP 4 loop of 4 minor chords (i–VI–III–VII or i–iv), Acoustic Bass 32 or Fingered 33 doubling the kick.
- A dusty feel: low velocities on the keys, a vinyl-noise bed from the synth engine, the drums slightly louder than everything.

## Trap (`trap`) · 130–150 BPM, felt as half-time · TR-808 kit (bank 128 preset 25)
```
kick    x......x..x.....      snare/clap  ........x.......  (the half-time backbeat on beat 3)
hats    xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx  with 1/32 or triplet rolls into beats 3 and 1
```
- **808 bass:** long notes following the kick (Synth Bass 38 held, or a synth sine with a pitch drop); the bass is the melody's floor.
- Minor or phrygian melody on a bell or pluck (Music Box 10, Glockenspiel 9, Pizzicato 45), sparse and repeated.

## Lo-fi (`lofi`) · 70–85 BPM · swing 58–62% · Brush or Jazz kit (bank 128 preset 40 or 32)
```
kick    x.....x...x.....      snare  ....o.......o...      hat  x.x.x.x.x.x.x.x.  (soft)
```
- EP 4 or 5 with maj7, min9 and 13 chords, a soft bass 33, a vinyl crackle and tape-wobble bed. Low velocities throughout.

## R&B / neo-soul (`rnb`) · 65–80 BPM · half-time, swung · Standard kit, soft
- Rim 37 on 2 and 4, finger-snap layer (clap 39 at low velocity), fretless bass 35 with slides, EP 4/5 with extended chords.

## House / dance (`house`) · 120–126 BPM · straight
```
kick    x...x...x...x...      clap  ....x.......x...      open hat  ..x...x...x...x.
```
- Offbeat bass, piano 0 stabs on the "and"s, a string 48 pad swelling into the drop.

## Uplifting corporate (`uplifting-corporate`) · 100–120 BPM · major
- Light kit (claps instead of snares), steel 25 or nylon 24 guitar strumming 8ths, piano 0 arpeggio, strings 48/49 pad,
  glockenspiel 9 sparkle on the hero line. Progression I–V–vi–IV. Build: add a layer every 4 bars.

## Cinematic / trailer (`cinematic`) · 60–90 BPM (hybrid action at 120+)
- Low strings 48 ostinato in 8ths, horns 60 or brass 61 long notes, timpani 47 and Taiko 116 on the big beats,
  choir 52 for awe, Orchestra Hit 55 layered with a synth boom for stings. Orchestra kit (bank 128 preset 48) for
  rolls. Minor key; i–VI–III–VII or a pedal note with rising chords. A riser and a silence right before the drop.

## Emotional piano / documentary (`documentary`) · 60–76 BPM
- Grand piano 0, sparse: a melody in the right hand, open fifths or tenths in the left. Warm pad 89 and cello 42 enter
  at the turn. Hold notes by their lengths (do not rely on the sustain pedal; not every renderer supports it).

## Cartoon / playful orchestra (`playful`) · 110–140 BPM
- Pizzicato strings 45, clarinet 71, bassoon 70, tuba 58, xylophone 13, marimba 12, glockenspiel 9, woodblocks
  (drum notes 76 and 77). Bouncy I–IV–V–I in a major key, staccato notes, big dynamic contrasts; it pairs with the
  comedy recipes in action-sounds.md.

## Luxury / premium (`luxury`) · 60–80 BPM
- No drums or only brushes, nylon guitar 24 or piano 0 with maj7 and 9 chords, a soft string 49 pad, lots of space and
  a long reverb. Fewer notes, longer notes.

## Moods → keys and progressions (quick picks)
| Mood | Progression | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| happy, welcoming | I–V–vi–IV, I–IV–V | major, mid tempo |
| warm, nostalgic | I–iii–IV–iv | the minor iv is the "sigh" |
| serious, mysterious | i–VI–III–VII, i–iv | natural minor |
| tense | i over a held low pedal, chromatic steps | no resolution until the payoff |
| triumphant | IV–V–I with a held final I | brass and timpani on the I |
| dreamy | Imaj7–IVmaj7 | slow, wide voicings |
