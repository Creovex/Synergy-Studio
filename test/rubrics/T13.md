# T13 rubric: look from reference

Frozen at L3. Never edited after that. One judgement per reference video (onescan, haura, motion-reel,
doodle-timeline, cosmos), plus the falsifier.

## Evidence
- Reference: `reference/breakdowns/sheets/<name>.jpg`.
- Build: `stills/look-card.jpg` and `look-reference.json` from `look --from reference/videos/<name>.mp4`.
- Falsifier: the look card of the built in `paper` look, judged against the cosmos sheet.

## Items
| Id | What | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| palette | background, ink and accent colours of the card against the reference | unrelated hues or light and dark swapped | background right, accents off, or the reverse | background, text and accents all recognisably taken from the reference |
| texture | grain, paper, flatness or gloss of the card against the reference | opposite (clean card for a grainy reference, or the reverse) | partly right | the surface quality of the card matches the reference |

## Pass
For each of the five references, both items at least 4. The falsifier passes when the `paper` card against the
cosmos sheet scores 2 or less on `palette`.

## How to judge

You are a fresh judge. Use only the evidence files named here. Score each item from 1 to 5 against the
anchors, cite the tiles or frames you looked at, and return one JSON object per item:
`{"item": "<id>", "result": <1..5 or TRUE/FALSE>, "evidence": "<one or two sentences>", "frame_refs": ["<file>#<tile or time>"]}`.
Return a JSON array and nothing else. Judge the style, not the subject: the build is about a different topic
on purpose. A score is final for the work you were shown.

Contact sheet tiles are numbered from 1, left to right, top to bottom. A reference sheet is made by
`ffmpeg` at L3 as `reference/breakdowns/sheets/<name>.jpg`: 12 evenly spaced frames, 4 columns.
