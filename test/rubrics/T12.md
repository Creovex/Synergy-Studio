## Items
| Id | What | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ink | black ink outlines of uneven weight, hatched shading, pencil grain on flat fills | clean vector art | outlines or hatching, not both | uneven ink lines with hatching and grain |

| overall | same family at a glance | clearly different | related in parts | same family |

## Guard rails (TRUE or FALSE)
| Id | TRUE when |
|---|---|
| not_known_character | the character does not resemble any known mascot or character, including the one in the reference |
| review_recorded | `feedback.md` records the build's own frame check that no character resembles a known mascot |

## Pass
Every guard rail TRUE and `overall` at least 4, with no other item below 3.

## How to judge

You are a fresh judge. Use only the evidence files named here. Score each item from 1 to 5 against the
anchors, cite the tiles or frames you looked at, and return one JSON object per item:
`{"item": "<id>", "result": <1..5 or TRUE/FALSE>, "evidence": "<one or two sentences>", "frame_refs": ["<file>#<tile or time>"]}`.
Return a JSON array and nothing else. Judge the style, not the subject: the build is about a different topic
on purpose. A score is final for the work you were shown.

Contact sheet tiles are numbered from 1, left to right, top to bottom. A reference sheet is made by
`ffmpeg` at L3 as `reference/breakdowns/sheets/<name>.jpg`: 12 evenly spaced frames, 4 columns.
